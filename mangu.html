Our Story & Purpose

Bridging Education & Impact at Zetech Mang'u

Sponsored by Rotary International (District 9212), the Rotaract Club of Zetech Mang'u brings together energetic students and young professionals dedicated to creating positive change within our campus and the surrounding community.

Based at the vibrant Zetech University Mang'u Campus, our club empowers members to address real-world challenges through sustainable community service while sharpening leadership skills, building professional networks, and forming lifelong friendships.

Our Mission

To provide opportunities for young people to address community needs, build professional skills, and foster fellowship.

Our Vision

A world where youth actively drive impactful social change and sustainable development through service.

What We Do

Rotary Areas of Focus

Our club aligns its initiatives with Rotary International's key causes to maximize sustainable impact.

Environmental Action

Tree planting drives around Mang'u, environmental awareness campaigns, and sustainable campus initiatives.

Education & Literacy

Mentorship programs for local schools, book donations, and youth empowerment talks across Kiambu County.

Professional Growth

Career talks, CV building workshops, public speaking practice, and networking with seasoned Rotarians.

Health & Hygiene

Medical camps, sanitary towel distribution drives, and mental health awareness workshops for students.

Community Economic Dev.

Supporting vulnerable families, children's home visits, and local community outreach drives.

Peacebuilding & Conflict

Fostering diversity, inclusivity, and peaceful conflict resolution among university students.

Guiding Principles

The Rotary Four-Way Test

Of the things we think, say, or do:

1st

Is it the
TRUTH?

2nd

Is it
FAIR to all concerned?

3rd

Will it build
GOODWILL & Better Friendships?

4th

Will it be
BENEFICIAL to all concerned?
